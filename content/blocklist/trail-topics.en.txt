# Rabbit Trail only (milestone e). The daily pipeline keeps whole Vital-Articles sections out (wars and
# military, crime, politics and government, military technology, drugs); a trail stop can be any article,
# so these category rules do that job for the trail. They match categories that say what an article IS
# (a war, a battle, a weapon, an election), never biographical ones ("… personnel of …", "People of …"),
# in line with the narrow-rules decision of 2026-10-06. One case-insensitive regular expression per line.

^(weapons|firearms|rifles|pistols|machine guns|missiles|bombs|artillery|tanks|warships|military vehicles|military aircraft|military equipment|ammunition)( of| by| in| used|$)
^(crimes|murders|organized crime|criminal organizations|kidnappings|robberies|assassinations)( in| of| by|$)
^(elections|referendums|political parties|political organizations)( in| of)\b
^(drugs|psychoactive drugs|recreational drugs)( of| in|$)
^(territorial disputes|disputed territories|political disputes|sovereignty disputes|separatism)( of| in| between| involving|$)
^(military installations|military bases|military airbases|air force bases|royal air force stations|naval bases|army bases|military units|military organizations)( of| in| by|$)
